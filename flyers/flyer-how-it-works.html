Inside the Automatic
Plant Care System

Sense → Score → Water
Runs on a BBC micro:bit

What’s in the build

  • BBC micro:bit + robot:bit boardThe brain. Runs the MicroPython program and drives the pump motor.
  • Soil moisture probeAnalog reading (0–1023), calibrated in dry and wet soil.
  • Light + temperature sensingBuilt into the micro:bit — no extra parts needed.
  • Pump + clear tubingDelivers a 3-second burst of water straight to the soil.
  • 3D-printed frameHolds the controller and routes the tubing beside the pot.

The control loop repeats every 10 seconds

1 · ReadSoil moisture, light level and temperature.
→
2 · CompareCheck each reading against the plant’s preferred level.
→
3 · ScoreAverage the three sub-scores into one 0–100 health score.
→
4 · ActSoil too dry? Run the pump. Then scroll the status on the LEDs.

How the health score works

Moisture
too dryideal ±10too wet
Light
too dimideal ±50too bright
Temperature
too coldideal ±10 °Ctoo hot

Inside the ideal band a factor scores 100. Outside it, the score drops by how far the reading is from the target. The overall score is the average of all three.

What the display shows

Score 82–100GOOD
Score 50–81OK
Score below 50POOR

Each factor also reports a plain-language status like “too dry” or “too dim”.
