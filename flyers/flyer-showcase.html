How it works

1

Sense

Every 10 seconds a soil probe, light sensor and thermometer take a fresh reading.

2

Score

Readings are compared with your plant’s ideal levels to give a 0–100 health score: good, ok or poor.

3

Water

Too dry? The pump switches on for 3 seconds and sends water through the tube. Only when it’s needed.
